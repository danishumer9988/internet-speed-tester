About Internet Speed Test

A fast, private, no-account internet speed test that measures your real connection quality in your browser.

What we measure

  • Download and upload throughput in Mbps
  • Ping (lowest latency) and jitter
  • Packet loss percentage
  • Test duration and time of day

How we do it

  • Tests run against Cloudflare's global edge network
  • Adaptive parallelism for accurate results on any speed
  • Warm-up trimmed to remove TCP slow-start bias

Privacy first

  • No account, no login, no ads
  • Anonymous results only — never linked to your identity
  • IP-derived location stored, precise location optional

Privacy Policy

We collect the minimum amount of data needed to run a speed test and keep our statistics meaningful. No accounts, no tracking pixels, no ad IDs.

What we store

  • Speed measurements (download, upload, ping, jitter, packet loss)
  • Your public IP address and its approximate location (city, region, country)
  • Precise coordinates only if you grant geolocation permission
  • Network metadata: ISP and ASN
  • Device metadata: browser, OS, screen size, timezone, language
  • Test timestamp

What we never store

  • Your name, email, phone, or address
  • Any account credentials (we don't have accounts)
  • Browsing history or data from other sites

Cookies & local storage

  • We use local storage to remember your theme and recent tests
  • We use a single cookie to remember your cookie-consent choice
  • No third-party advertising or tracking cookies

Where it goes

  • Test results are stored anonymously in our database
  • Speed measurements use Cloudflare's public test endpoints
  • IP lookups use ipapi.co — your IP is sent to ipapi.co for geolocation

Terms of Use

This service is provided as-is, for personal and diagnostic use. Measurements are estimates and can vary depending on network conditions, server load, and device performance.

Fair use

  • Do not abuse the API endpoints or attempt to overwhelm the service
  • Do not use automated scripts to submit fraudulent test results
  • Do not rely on results for legal, contractual, or billing claims

No warranty

  • Results are informational and may differ from your ISP's measurements
  • We are not responsible for decisions made based on the results
  • The service may be changed or discontinued at any time
